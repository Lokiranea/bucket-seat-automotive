Nexora annual vehicle safety inspection

Due for your inspection?

Most vehicles in Nexora must pass a safety inspection once every 12 months. After passing, your vehicle receives a new inspection sticker for the windshield. Bradley books inspections at certified centers only.

What is inspected

  • Brakes and parking brake
  • Headlights, signal lights and horn
  • Steering and suspension
  • Tires, wheels and rims
  • Mirrors, windshield, glass and wipers
  • Exhaust, fuel system and emissions equipment
  • Seats, seat belts and airbags
  • Doors, hood latch, floor pan and license plate light

If your vehicle does not pass

  • A rejection sticker or notice may be issued.
  • Repair the listed defects.
  • Return for reinspection within 15 days.

Driving without a valid inspection sticker may lead to a ticket.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change my service center location?

Yes. You can usually change locations before final confirmation, though you may need to reselect available services or times.

Will I get a confirmation email or SMS?

Yes. A confirmation is typically sent by email, and optional text reminders may be available.

What time should I arrive for my service?

Arrive about 10 to 15 minutes early so check-in is quick and easy.

Are loaner vehicles available during service?

Loaner availability varies by location and service type. Check with your service center when booking.

Can I book if I'm not sure what my vehicle needs?

Yes. You can choose a general inspection or diagnostic visit and discuss symptoms with the technician.
